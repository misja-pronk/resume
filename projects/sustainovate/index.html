    SUSTAINOVATE

    Designing a Data Marketplace for Shipping Companies

    Role Solution Architect
    Duration 2 MONTHS
    Via Macaw
    Status ✓ DELIVERED

    Sustainovate is a data processing company for shipping companies. It creates data solutions — reports and metrics — that are shared between shipping companies, environmental organizations and universities. Their existing data platform was not designed to meet new privacy, accountability and security requirements.

    I was responsible for identifying the requirements, creating the use cases and designing the technical solution.
