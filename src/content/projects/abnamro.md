ABN AMRO had a number of applications and systems hosted in the UK that needed to be relocated before Brexit: retiring the SAS solution used for pension consolidation, migrating on-premises client-server Microsoft Access applications to Azure and migrating the ABN AMRO customer portal to Azure.

My task was to design the new cloud architecture for the migration, make agreements with suppliers regarding the replacement of SAS, monitor all implementations and take responsibility for the quality of the solution.
