The Van Gogh Museum wanted to gain insight into the behavior of its visitors. This information was stored across various systems, so a central data platform had to be developed.

I developed the ETL for the local systems and APIs, provisioned and configured the Azure environments, trained the staff and was responsible for the implementation of the project.
