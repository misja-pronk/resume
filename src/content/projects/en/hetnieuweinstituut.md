Het Nieuwe Instituut has an obligation to report to the Dutch government on the progress and budgets of long-term projects. They wanted a data platform and accompanying reports to support this.

I developed the ETL for the local systems (AFAS and Exact), implemented and configured the Azure environment and was responsible for the realization of the entire project.
