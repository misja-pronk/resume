Henkel is a German manufacturer of beauty and care products, among others. There was a need for a central data platform incorporating API sources such as Google Analytics, Searchmetrics and Facebook. Henkel launched a pilot project to investigate the feasibility of this new platform.

I was responsible for defining the pilot, implementing the Searchmetrics ELT and leading the development team of 7 developers.
